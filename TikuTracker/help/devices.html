Devices

A board reaches this desktop over a cable or over a network, and is mounted under /devices once you connect it from the Devices menu on the top bar.

Device Monitor

The monitor samples a connected board every five seconds and draws what it read. A reading the firmware does not publish is shown as Unavailable, never as a zero.

VFS Inspector

The inspector browses a device's namespace. Only audited settings can be edited, and every edit is reviewed and confirmed before it is written.
