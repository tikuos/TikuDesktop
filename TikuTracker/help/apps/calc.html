Calculator

The Deskbar row is Calculator, under Applications. It is a pocket calculator: a display and a keypad with the digits, a point, the four operations, equals, C, ± and %.

Operations bind left to right, as a pocket calculator's do: 2 + 3 × 4 = shows 20. An operator pressed straight after another replaces it. ± changes the sign of the entry and % divides it by a hundred, so 200 × 50 % = shows 100.

The keyboard works too: the digits, ., + - * /, Return for equals, Backspace to take back a digit, and Escape for C.

Dividing by zero shows Error, and only C answers until it is pressed. Calculator > Copy result puts the display on the desktop clipboard. For exact unsigned words, bit fields and checksums there is the Developer Calculator.
