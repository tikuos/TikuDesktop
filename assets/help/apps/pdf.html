TikuPDF

A reader for the PDFs this desk works with: datasheets, reference manuals and papers. Choose Applications > TikuPDF, or double-click a PDF in Tracker; PDFs (.pdf, in any case) open in TikuPDF unless File Associations names another reader. TikuPDF also appears in Open With.

Opening a document

Choose Open or press Ctrl+O. The document opens on a background worker, so a datasheet of a thousand pages is ready in a moment and the window never waits on it. If a file cannot be opened the status line says why — not a PDF, encrypted, or damaged beyond repair — and the document already shown stays.

Reading

  • Pages follow one another; scroll with the mouse wheel, the arrow keys, Page Up and Page Down, or Space and Shift+Space. Home and End go to the first and last page.
  • Width fits the pages to the window's width and follows it as the window is resized; Page fits a whole page. + and - (or Ctrl+mouse wheel) step the zoom from 25% to 400%; Ctrl+0 shows the actual size.
  • Type a page number in the field beside the arrows and press Enter to go to it. < and > step a page.
  • Pages are drawn as you reach them; a page not drawn yet is white, and after a zoom the old drawing stands in until the new one is ready.

Outline and links

Outline shows the document's own table of contents beside the pages; click an entry to go there, or its + and - to fold it. The entry for the part you are reading stays marked as you scroll. Links in the page work too: the status line says where one leads as the pointer passes over it, and a click follows it. Ctrl+[ goes back to where you were. A link to a web address is copied to the clipboard instead, since no browser is started from here.

Finding and copying

Ctrl+F goes to the Find field. Enter searches the whole document, a stretch of pages at a time, so you can read on while it looks; the first match after where you are is shown as soon as it is found. Enter or Ctrl+G goes to the next match, Shift+Enter or Shift+Ctrl+G to the previous. Case, and the different kinds of space, hyphen and quote a typeset page uses, do not matter; a phrase broken over two lines is still found.

Drag across text to select it and press Ctrl+C to copy it. Ctrl+A selects the text of the page you are on. The clipboard holds 4 KB; copying more takes the first 4 KB and the status line says so.

What is not drawn yet

Shaded fills and patterns are drawn in flat grey, and faxed, JPEG 2000 and JBIG2 pictures as grey boxes, so the page keeps its layout. Encrypted PDFs, forms and annotations other than links are not read yet. Fonts a PDF names without carrying are drawn with the desktop's own faces, at the widths the document gives them.
