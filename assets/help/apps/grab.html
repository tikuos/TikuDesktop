TikuGrab

Takes a picture of the whole desk, or of one window alone, for a bug report or a document. Choose Applications > TikuGrab.

What to grab

The list at the left offers Whole desk first and then every window open on the desk, the front one first; choose one and press Grab (the camera, Ctrl+G, or Return). TikuGrab's own window is never in the picture: the desk is drawn without it.

A window is drawn by itself, so all of it is in the picture even where another window covers it on the desk. Its rounded corners and the space beside its tab are transparent, so the picture sits cleanly on a page of any colour.

Waiting before grabbing

To catch a menu that is open or a tip that is showing, choose a delay with the timer button or the Delay menu: 3, 5 or 10 seconds. Press Grab, open the menu or point at the button, and wait: the status line counts down, and so does an icon beside the Deskbar's clock.

The target is fixed when you press Grab. Selecting another item or refreshing the list during the countdown does not change it. If that window closes, the capture fails; TikuGrab does not capture the whole desk instead. Use Cancel Capture, Escape, or a click on the icon beside the clock to cancel the countdown, and the first two to stop waiting. A request already sent to the desktop may still save a PNG in Screenshots. After 30 seconds without a reply, you can try again; late replies do not replace a newer capture.

Where pictures go

Every picture is saved at once as a PNG in the Screenshots folder on the desktop, named after what it shows and the time: tiku-desk-..., tiku-window-.... The same folder holds the pictures Take Screenshot makes (the camera at the right of the menu bar, or Shift+Ctrl+3). They are compressed: a whole desk takes a few hundred kilobytes, not megabytes.

The last picture is shown in the window. File > Save As... (Ctrl+S) keeps a copy somewhere else; Open in TikuImage (Ctrl+O) shows it full size; Show in Tracker opens the Screenshots folder.

Preview decoding, resizing, and Save As run in the background. The window list supports the scrollbar, mouse wheel, and arrow keys. Narrow windows put the list above the preview and wrap the toolbar.

Save As writes a temporary file first and replaces the destination only after writing succeeds. Saving onto the original screenshot, or a link to the same file, leaves it unchanged. A symbolic link to a different file is refused; choose the actual destination file instead.
