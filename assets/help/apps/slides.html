TikuSlides

Shows PowerPoint presentations (.pptx) a slide at a time, and presents them. Choose Applications > TikuSlides, or double-click a presentation in Tracker; presentations open in TikuSlides unless File Associations names another tool. TikuSlides also appears in Open With.

Looking through

Each slide is drawn as its master, layout and theme say: its background and the pictures and shapes behind the text, titles and bullet points at their levels, numbered points, tables in their style, pictures cut to their shapes, groups turned and flipped, and slide numbers. Text that PowerPoint shrank to fit its box is shrunk the same. Hidden slides are left out, as PowerPoint leaves them out of a PDF.

Slides fit the window whole. Scroll through them, or use the arrows on the toolbar and the Go menu; Zoom In and Zoom Out (Ctrl with + and -) step the size, and Fit Width in the View menu fills the window's width. The slides' titles are listed down the left; click one to go to its slide, and View > Outline hides the list or shows it again.

Presenting

View > Present (Shift+Ctrl+P, or the easel on the toolbar) gives the window to one slide on a black ground. The Right and Down arrows, Space, Page Down, Return and a click go to the next slide; the Left and Up arrows, Page Up and Backspace go back; Home and End go to the first and the last. Escape stops presenting. Make the window as large as the desk allows before you begin.

Finding, copying and exporting

The Find field (Ctrl+F), copying (Ctrl+C) and File > Export as PDF (Ctrl+E) work as they do in TikuDoc: a presentation exported is a PDF of its slides, a page each, its titles in the outline.

What is not shown yet

Animations and transitions, video and sound, and speaker notes are not shown. SmartArt diagrams are drawn as PowerPoint last laid them out, and charts from the values PowerPoint last saved with them; surface charts and the newer kinds (waterfall, treemap, funnel and the like) are grey boxes of their size. Shadows, glows and 3-D effects, 3-D charts among them, are drawn flat. PowerPoint 97-2003 presentations (.ppt) and presentations locked with a password are not opened.
