TikuDoc

Reads Word documents (.docx) and OpenDocument text (.odt) as the pages they lay out to. Choose Applications > TikuDoc, or double-click a document in Tracker; documents open in TikuDoc unless File Associations names another tool. TikuDoc also appears in Open With.

Reading

A document is laid out afresh on this computer, as its styles say: its fonts, sizes and colours, paragraphs and their spacing, lists and their numbers, tables with their borders and shading, pictures in the text and beside it, text boxes and shapes, headers and footers with their page numbers, footnotes at the foot of their page and endnotes at the end, watermarks, and a table of contents whose entries go to their headings. A font the document names but this computer lacks is drawn in one of the same widths, so lines break where they did.

The pages scroll one under another. Zoom In and Zoom Out (Ctrl with + and -) step the size, Ctrl+0 shows them at their actual size, and Fit Width and Fit Page in the View menu and on the toolbar size them to the window. The arrow keys, Page Up, Page Down, Space, Home and End move through them; the Go menu goes a page on or back, or to the first or last. A link out of the document is copied to the clipboard; a link inside it goes there, and Back (Ctrl+[) returns.

The document's headings are listed down the left: click one, or choose it with the arrow keys and press Return, to go there. View > Outline, or the button beside the PDF one, hides the list and shows it again. A document without headings shows no list.

Finding and copying

Type in the Find field (Ctrl+F) and press Return to find the next place it appears, Shift+Return for the one before; Ctrl+G and Shift+Ctrl+G do the same from anywhere. Every match is marked. Drag across text to select it and press Ctrl+C to copy it; Ctrl+A selects the text of the page in view.

Export as PDF

File > Export as PDF (Ctrl+E, or the PDF button on the toolbar) writes the pages you see as a PDF, beside the document or where you choose. Its fonts go in with it, cut to the letters used; its links and outline are kept; its text can be searched and copied in any PDF reader.

What is not shown yet

A document with tracked changes is shown as it would read with every change accepted; comments are not shown. SmartArt diagrams are drawn as Word last laid them out, and charts from the values Word last saved with them; surface charts and the newer kinds (waterfall, treemap, funnel and the like) are grey boxes of their size, as are pictures in the old Windows metafile formats. Equations are set on one line, fractions written with a slash. Fields other than page numbers show what Word last put in them. Right-to-left text is not laid out right to left. Word 97-2003 documents (.doc), rich text (.rtf) and documents locked with a password are not opened.
